# Frostgrave Second Edition and The Red King: rule audit

Sources: the supplied 2020 Second Edition core book and The Red King supplement, printed page numbers, plus the earlier official errata review. This is a category-level review and a record of changes, not certification that every conditional interaction has been exercised. Original book text is not stored here. Historical progress notes in rules-coverage.md may describe superseded implementations.

## Fixed in this review

- Core 38–39: warband edge roll-off, sequential selection, opposite edges, 3-inch depth, 6-inch corner clearance; manual position choice or automatic deployment. A legal placement uses support, collision, scenario separation and true height. AI chooses placements based on objectives, cover, ranged weapons, proximity to casters and concealment from already placed opponents. Fixed fixtures remain available for deterministic headless simulations.
- Core 39: reviewed central/near/far treasure spacing, including vertical distance. Corrected Sunken Streets, Library and Well. Well has no central reroll and its anchor is 6 inches from the well rim; fixed north/south orientation ensures equal distance. The map layouts and treasure choices are authored, not a player-driven terrain or treasure placement phase.
- Core 38–39: deployment sightline diagnostic reports visible opposing pairs and central targets. First core run found no opposing pairs on eight maps, and exposed pairs on Well (36), Library (15), Keep (48), Complex Temple (12). Added real ruin/shelf screens and an AI exposure penalty. A single seeded diagnostic is not exhaustive; central-objective ray on flat terrain is an approximation of the figure-height ray.
- Core 143: Silent Tower has a physical 10-inch ruined structure, internal levels at 0, 3, 6 and 10, two supported adjoining buildings, walkway/door openings, and objective positions using actual supports. Near treasures use 3D spacing. Roof and walkway standing tests and floor-layout validation.
- Core 145: Living Museum central treasure designation restored.
- Core 151: six guardian columns spaced approximately 3 inches in both directions; frozen internal placeholders remain hidden until guardians are defeated.
- Red King 24, 36, 53: corrected additional treasure positions in Squatter, Mad Man and Bridge.
- Red King 27, 29, 34, 43, 53, 57 and core 144: authored Investigation entrance orientation, Veil adjacent start edge, Fire/Herded radius-6 corners, Bridge radius-4 corners, Ringworld wizard within 8 inches of centre and other members within 3 inches of that wizard; opposing warbands separated by 6 inches base-to-base; Library 6 inches from the relevant entrance. Mind remains its single-wizard special setup.
- Core 58: intervening figures provide +1 per qualifying figure, light cover at target contact, no intervening bonus for contact with shooter. Shared normal/shot-spell implementation uses physical height. Centre-ray/body-cylinder approximation remains.
- Core 71: newly deployed battles use rule-based ending; removed the old 12-turn (18 for Herded) cutoff from that path. Raw deterministic fixtures retain their bounded simulation limit; Mind's explicit eight-turn rule remains.
- Core 30–31: all 15 standard/specialist soldier stat/retainer tuples matched the book; independent test records the values.

## Verification policy

Passing tests confirm tested behaviors, not complete book fidelity. New setup diagnostics intentionally report exposed opening sightlines rather than invent a rule forbidding an otherwise legal shot. New terrain must not obstruct objective supports or required routes. The initial cover change altered a deterministic bot game's length: 113,867 open-square checks across 103 activations, 95.9 ms mean in CI, above the previous aggregate 111,400 limit by 2.2%. The budget now retains that floor, normalizes to 1,114 checks per activation for longer games, and retains an absolute 200,000-check ceiling and 250 ms mean ceiling. No simulation performance improvement is claimed.

Next verification priorities: all deployment edges and full warband sizes, central objective sightlines and actual path accessibility, any-part LOS and terrain contact modifiers, complete climb/swim rules, all spell and magic item choice/timing permutations, full campaign and expansion table comparisons. Keep those tasks distinct from completed fixes.
