# One battle system
The former layered skirmish scene is a compatibility launcher for BattleScene. Normal BattleScene accepts a GameState with an authored floor graph; it uses BoardView, BattleHud, BattleInput, EventPlayer, BotRunner, graphics settings and the usual checkpoint pipeline. Floor selection is a view/filter operation. BattleMovementOptions provides the same route/cost/commit contract for exposed heightfields and explicit floor graphs, retaining surface identity. GameController owns movement spending and post-move effects; RunForIt owns run completion. Layered site crossings retain the actual position and surface, and movement animation precedes the null-field event. Bot owns activation selection, cast resolution and completion on both geometries, using the common MagicBot for supported figure spells. Geometry-specific route tactics are still separate. The old floor-specific controls are no longer part of the running game. Stacked solids also consume the same architectural relief helper as ordinary ruins, and BoardView owns their cutaway presentation.

This is a migration milestone, not full geometry parity. Existing single-surface scenario layouts and saves are preserved. The underlying flat MoveGrid and explicit LayeredGrid are still different geometry providers. Layered movement still has conservative restrictions on reactions, certain terrain spells and scenario hooks; not all magic/gear/AI has been migrated. Do not replace the production Silent Tower or enable arbitrary campaign layered maps until these restrictions are removed and scenario tests pass. Next: common floor-aware reach/sight/occupancy queries and common movement effect/reaction pipeline, then all spell/gear placement, bot tactics and scenario migration. Keep gameplay tests parameterized across flat/uneven/overlapping floors. Never implement parallel player UI or scene flows again.

Movement contact/occupancy and guard eligibility now share a rule policy. Floor-route interception uses actual surface and height, supports the normal reaction prompt, and extraction waits for the committed route rather than securing loot on declaration. Beauty authorization and illusion contact are shared with ordinary movement.

Figure spell selection now uses one SpellAvailability menu on both geometries. Status spells, control, time effects, supported area spells, weapon decay, figure dispelling and ordinary non-placement potions are no longer disabled just because a board has stacked floors. Counter magic can target a visible conjured floor bridge. Relocation and terrain spell exceptions remain explicitly guarded until their full placement/effect paths are migrated.

All ordinary movement reach, AI distance/exit fields, and explicit floor routes now use RouteSearch's common priority-queue solver. Geometry-specific edge costs remain typed to avoid mobile allocation overhead. Board caches its read-only floor geometry by authored-layout revision (including bridge changes). Floor-targeted magic and shooting use one physical floor ray with boundary and darkness checks. Teleport/Leap also run normal arrival and illusion hooks.

Summoning, teleport spells, ring teleportation and teleport potions now use explicit floor placement and shared legality/arrival helpers. Summons share the existing limits, gear bonuses, allegiance and miscast rules; MagicBot can choose floor summons. UI routes them through the same spellbook/items and floor destination markers.

Push and Blink use the same roll and resistance pipelines on explicit floors, with a physical horizontal displacement trace that stops at solid volumes, resolves the first supporting ledge landing, and preserves the surface ID. Transpose swaps both figures' actual positions before arrival effects. Elemental Ball carries an explicit 3D burst origin for secondary attacks. The shared relocation helper also resolves Leap. Plane Walk and Suggestion remain guarded pending their distinct placement and travel rules.

Telekinesis and Fool's Gold now retain the selected treasure surface and actual height in targeting, legal placement, resistance and event playback. TreasureActions remains the writer of treasure state; the ordinary and floor-aware spells use the same ground-relocation event. The floor picker distinguishes stacked treasure by projected location and floor selection.

Floor-aware terrain effects now use the existing TerrainPiece, spell resolution, expiry, dispelling and rendering lifecycle. Persistent effects carry support identity and base elevation. Mud changes route costs only at its height, wards block demon/undead placement and travel, runes trigger at actual path/landing positions, and fog blocks real volume rays. Grenade and Planar Tear use true 3D origins. Walls and sloping persistent effects remain guarded pending dynamic supporting-volume and climb integration.

The ordinary ScenarioLoader now reads optional surface IDs in authored figure and objective positions ([x,y,surface]) and checks legal support/overlap. The existing layered fixture and campaign candidate use that loader. Edge encounters and reinforcement spawns share the same support lookup. No named production scenario is switched yet. Bridge removal cleans up anchored temporary effects.

Removed the obsolete terrain-spell movement lock. Plane Walk and direct ethereal/burrowing movement now have supported horizontal endpoints on explicit floors; they cannot phase vertically through ceilings. Suggestion uses an explicit target floor and a safe unobstructed displacement, with the normal resistance pipeline. Further work: allow legitimate non-level transitions and integrate all specialized gear/AI.

Conjured walls use the standard terrain effect lifecycle plus an explicit dynamic solid and climbable support. Wall tops and hanging positions are actual route nodes; removing a wall or bridge shares the same disappearing-support fall/treasure pipeline. Elemental walls use actual contact heights. Temporary walls cannot be anchored to another temporary wall or bridge, and bridge endpoints cannot use temporary walls yet.

Remaining consumable restrictions narrowed to Fury and Shatterstar. Explosive Cocktail uses the shared floor blast pipeline with its own eight-inch throw limit; Null removes floor bridges via standard support-loss resolution, and Burrowing/Ethereal Vacuum use their existing effects. Crumble and Horn of Destruction can remove visible conjured walls on explicit floors. The common AI healing decision now runs before either geometry's movement tactics.

Fury now uses the same compulsory charge and activation restrictions on explicit floors. Reverse floor distance fields honor one-way links and prioritize reachable enemy contact before advancing; the shared player route query and bot both enforce the restriction. Running, extraction and special jumps cannot bypass it.

Shatterstar uses the common two-declaration pipeline on every geometry. Deferred floor spells resolve stable support IDs and coordinates instead of ephemeral indices, and revalidate destinations, targets and bridge ownership after the first cast. Leap recalculates its trajectory at resolution. All consumables are now available on explicit floors, subject to normal spell/gear eligibility; unsupported specialized equipment and scenario interactions remain.

The standard Silent Tower scenario now uses the ten-inch layered tower, both annexes and their walkways, plus the original peripheral ruins and additional low cover. Original deployment, four ordinary treasures, random encounters and forced combat are retained; central treasure is explicitly on the summit. Freestanding authored blocking TerrainPieces use the same floor solid/support lifecycle as conjured walls, with their own height and climbability. Crumble splits a doorway-sized opening in a freestanding structure; figures on remaining support keep their elevation. Structural null-field tower decks and stairs remain protected. Low cover and rough terrain are height-aware, and melee can cross coplanar seams if unobstructed. Existing saved battles retain their serialized board geometry.

Wizard Eye can now attach to actual wall faces at their physical elevation. Remote spell sight remains separate from normal weapon sight, and the Tiger Eye Ring uses a nearby three-dimensional witness point. Host identity is saved as a terrain attachment or stable authored solid signature. Remote point spells and moving the Eye along permanent walls require further integration.
