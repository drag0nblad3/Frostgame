# Convex architecture

Floor kits and direct floor layouts accept `convex_solids` entries with an ordered convex planar `profile` (3D [x,y,z] vertices) and an `extrusion` vector. Each prism computes exact convex-plane segment clipping and separating-axis overlap with the existing figure clearance box. The same faces build its rendered mesh. Quarter-turn assembly transforms and height offsets are supported. Arbitrary angles are authored directly in profile coordinates; mixed kits still use quarter-turn placement to preserve lattice-linked floors.

Silent Tower demonstrates an open octagonal ruin with varying wall heights, a twelve-segment masonry arch, diagonal wall remnants and sloping roof fragments over broken galleries. Rectangular walkable floor surfaces and ordinary wall/terrain spells are unchanged. Designated non-supporting straight wall prisms with a unique crumble_id are spell-targetable using the existing Crumble action. The spell removes a 1.5-inch central section along the actual wall axis; the remaining profiles retain their exact collision and sight geometry. Handles are serialized with TerrainPiece and rendered only by the floor geometry. Load-bearing arch, roof and deck destruction is still deferred, as are curved walkable surfaces and arbitrary rotations of linked floor assemblies.

Flat walkable surfaces can specify an ordered convex `polygon` and optional `deck_thickness`. The polygon is the actual support outline, its prism is generated automatically, and a base must fit inside the edges. The gallery decks now have cutaway corners. Convex-only unlinked kits can be placed with `angle_degrees`; linked or rectangular kits still use quarter turns. Arbitrary rotation of linked stairs and concave decks is not supported yet.
